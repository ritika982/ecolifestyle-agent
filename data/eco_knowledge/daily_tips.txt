DAILY ECO TIPS FOR INDIAN HOMES

Use pressure cooker instead of open pot saves 70 percent cooking gas.
Buy loose vegetables at sabji mandi with zero packaging.
Compost all vegetable peels fruit scraps eggshells tea leaves.
Fix dripping taps they waste 20 litres of water per day.
Switch all bulbs to LED uses 75 percent less electricity than CFL.
AC at 24 degrees instead of 18 degrees saves 24 percent electricity.
Unplug chargers and TVs when not in use standby uses electricity.
Walk or cycle for trips under 2 km saves fuel and reduces pollution.
Metro emits 70 to 80 percent less CO2 per passenger than private cars.
Carry a cloth bag everywhere prevents 700 plastic bags per year.
Buy second hand on OLX before buying new saves money and resources.
Repair before replacing India has excellent repair culture use it.
Use steel dabbas instead of plastic containers for food storage.
Install rainwater harvesting saves 30000 litres per year in monsoon.
Turn off tap while brushing teeth saves 6 litres per minute.
Use both sides of paper before recycling.
Solar water heater payback is 3 to 4 years then free hot water for 20 years.
Clean AC filters monthly dirty filter uses 15 percent more electricity.
Full loads only in washing machine half load uses same water as full load.
Wash vegetables in a bowl not under running tap saves water.
