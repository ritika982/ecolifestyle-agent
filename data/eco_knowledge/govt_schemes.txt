INDIAN GOVERNMENT ECO SCHEMES

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana launched 2024.
40 percent subsidy for rooftop solar up to 2 kW systems.
Maximum 78000 rupees subsidy per household.
300 units free electricity per month after installation.
Apply at pmsuryaghar.gov.in with Aadhaar and electricity account.
Available for any household with rooftop and electricity connection.

FAME II scheme for electric vehicles.
Maximum 1.5 lakh rupees subsidy on electric two-wheelers.
Subsidy is deducted upfront at the dealer no need to claim separately.
Check registered models at fame2.heavyindustries.gov.in.

PM KUSUM scheme for farmers.
90 percent subsidy on solar pumps for agriculture.
60 percent government grant and 30 percent bank loan.
Farmer pays only 10 percent of total cost.
Apply through State Agriculture Department or local DISCOM.

Swachh Bharat Mission Phase 2.
All cities must do door-to-door waste collection.
Waste segregation at source is mandatory across India.
Compost plants being set up in all major cities.

Plastic Waste Management Rules 2021.
Single use plastic ban from July 1 2022 strictly enforced.
Fines up to 1 lakh rupees for businesses violating ban.
Minimum plastic bag thickness now 75 micron.

E-Waste Management Rules 2022.
Producers must collect 60 percent of e-waste they generate.
Consumers can return e-waste to any authorised collection point.
Find authorised collectors at cpcb.nic.in website.

National Clean Air Programme NCAP.
Covers 132 cities with bad air quality across India.
4000 crore rupees allocated for air quality improvement.
Cities include Patna Delhi Mumbai Kanpur Lucknow Agra.

Bihar Jal Jeevan Hariyali Mission.
24000 crore rupees for ponds trees and solar irrigation in Bihar.
BREDA Bihar Renewable Energy Dev Agency handles solar applications.
PM KUSUM solar pumps available for Bihar farmers.

Delhi EV Policy.
30000 rupees subsidy on electric two-wheelers in Delhi.
Full road tax and registration fee waiver on EVs in Delhi.
500 public EV charging stations deployed across Delhi.

Karnataka EV Policy.
100 percent road tax waiver on all electric vehicles.
KREDL Karnataka Renewable Energy Dev Ltd handles solar subsidy.
Additional Karnataka subsidy on top of central PM Surya Ghar scheme.
