RECYCLING IN INDIA

Segregate waste into three bins at home.
Wet waste green bin vegetable peels food scraps tea leaves flowers.
Dry waste blue bin paper cardboard plastic bottles glass metal tins.
Sanitary waste wrap separately diapers medicine strips bandages.

Plastics with triangle number 1 PET like water bottles are most recycled.
Kabadiwala pays 8 to 15 rupees per kg for PET bottles.
Kabadiwala pays 8 to 12 rupees per kg for newspaper and paper.
Kabadiwala pays 80 to 120 rupees per kg for aluminium cans.
Kabadiwala pays 400 to 600 rupees per kg for copper wire.
Kabadiwala pays 50 to 500 rupees for old mobile phones.

E-waste like old mobiles laptops chargers must not go in dustbin.
Find authorised e-waste collectors on cpcb.nic.in website.
Attero Recycling operates across 800 cities in India.
Karo Sambhav is e-waste collection partner for 100 plus brands.
Major brands like Samsung Apple LG have take-back programs.
Airtel Jio Vodafone stores accept old phones for recycling.

Single use plastics banned from July 2022 in India.
Plastic bags below 75 micron thickness are banned.
Plastic straws cutlery plates cups thermocol decorations are banned.

Composting at home is easy.
Collect peels in pot with soil and dry leaves.
Add dry leaves or newspaper between layers to prevent smell.
Turn the pile every week with a stick.
In 45 to 60 days you get dark rich compost for your plants.

Kabadiwalla Connect app helps find your nearest kabadiwala.
India has 1.5 million kabadiwalas use them for all recyclables.
Kabadiwalas collect paper metal plastic glass and electronics.

Battery disposal is important.
Old batteries contain lead acid cadmium never put in regular dustbin.
Take to authorised battery dealer they are required to accept them.
Amaron Exide dealers accept old batteries.
Automobile service centres accept old car batteries.

Glass is 100 percent recyclable indefinitely without quality loss.
Remove lids before recycling glass bottles.
Broken glass wrap in newspaper before giving to kabadiwala.

Paper recycling tips.
Remove food residue before recycling paper.
Cardboard newspapers books notebooks all recyclable.
Wax coated paper and tissue paper are not recyclable.
